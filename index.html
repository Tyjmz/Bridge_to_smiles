About Bridge to Smiles

Every family deserves a clearer path to care.

Our mission

The mission of Bridge to Smiles is to improve access to affordable dental care for children and families by providing information and referral assistance to help individuals identify dentists who accept their insurance, locate low-cost dental clinics, schedule appointments, and access available transportation resources.

Why we started

Bridge to Smiles was founded on the belief that no child or family should go without needed care because they don't know where to turn.

We recognize that finding affordable dental care can be overwhelming, especially for families facing financial, transportation, or language barriers. Our mission is to make the process easier by offering guidance, connecting families with available resources, and helping them take the next step toward healthier smiles.

How we help

Support for the steps along the way

We offer information and referral guidance to help families explore available dental care resources.

Finding Dental Providers

We help families identify dental offices that accept their insurance.

Affordable Dental Care

We connect families with low-cost clinics and information about affordable care options.

Appointment Assistance

We help families navigate the process of arranging dental appointments.

Transportation Resources

We help families identify available transportation options and assistance when possible.

Available options vary by location and individual circumstances. We cannot guarantee provider availability, insurance acceptance, appointments, transportation, or financial assistance.
